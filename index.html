Mixing your colors…

DISCVERSE is an original, DISC-inspired reflection activity. It is not an official, licensed, or scientifically validated assessment, and it doesn't diagnose or predict anything. Your answers are processed locally in your browser and are never collected, transmitted, or stored on a server.

How your results work

The quiz

You answer 15 workplace scenarios. Every question has four answers, one for each style: Dominance (Red), Influence (Yellow), Steadiness (Green) and Conscientiousness (Blue). Answer order is shuffled so position never hints at a style, and no answer is better than another.

The scoring

Each answer you choose gives one point to the style it reflects. Your percentages are your points divided by 15, rounded so the four numbers add up to exactly 100%. Ties are decided on the underlying points, not the rounded percentages. If your top styles tie, DISCVERSE says so and lets you explore each one instead of picking a winner. The scoring matrix is the QUESTIONS list at the top of the script, so it is easy to review and edit.

What it is, and what it isn't

These percentages show how your answers were distributed in this activity. They are not psychometric scores, clinical results, or probabilities, and they can change from day to day, role to role, and mood to mood. Most people flex across all four colors. Use the results as a conversation starter, and read the person, not the label.

The communication guides

The "Talk to Another Color" tips use a simple four-style framework based on the widely used DISC approach, with a color shorthand. They are habits to try, not rules, and no pairing is better or worse than another.

Privacy

Everything happens in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere, and nothing is saved when you close the page. Your PNGs are generated on your device.
